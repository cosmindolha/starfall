# Asset notices

This repository preserves the assets used by the personal Starfall game. No general open-source or redistribution license is granted by this upload.

- **Ships:** original procedural Blender geometry, rendered into 23 bank views and packed into `assets/images/ships.png`. The renders were styled using the installed Pixelization-CoreML model. The project's original provenance records that model's terms as **non-commercial research use only**. Model weights, models and art tools are not included. The upload does not grant commercial rights to the styled atlas.
- **Backgrounds:** the planet and asteroid were generated through PixelLab; the final nebula was created with an image generator. Only the game-ready images are included.
- **Sound effects:** seventeen original, seeded procedural arcade effects, exported as WAV files.
- **Music:** the user-supplied *Commander Whiskers* soundtrack, converted to Ogg Vorbis for streaming. The original MP3 and its cover artwork are not included. No separate soundtrack license is granted.
- **Fonts:** Rajdhani Bold and Share Tech Mono Regular are bundled under the SIL Open Font License. Their complete license and attribution files are in `assets/fonts/Rajdhani-LICENSE.txt` and `assets/fonts/ShareTechMono-LICENSE.txt`.
